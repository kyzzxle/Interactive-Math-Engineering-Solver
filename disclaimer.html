Precision and rounding

  • Decimal results are usually rounded to six decimal places. Rounded values are marked as approximations; exact values (such as multiples of π or simplified square roots) are labelled as exact.
  • Computers store most decimals in binary floating point, which can introduce tiny errors of around one part in 1015. The calculators are written to keep these effects below the displayed precision.
  • Counting results are computed exactly using arbitrary-size integers. Results too large to compute exactly in a browser are shown in scientific notation and marked as approximate.
  • A result is only as good as its inputs. Check units, signs and which measurement you entered in each field.
