What the prism calculator does

A prism is a solid with two identical, parallel polygon faces (the bases) joined by rectangular side faces. Its cross-section is the same all the way along, which makes its volume simply "base area × height". Choose one of the supported prism types — a box, a cube, a triangular prism with any triangle as its base, or a prism on a regular polygon — and the calculator finds the base area, perimeter, lateral area, total surface area and volume.

Try changing the input

Change only the height: the volume and lateral area change in proportion, but the base area does not. Change a base edge and every result changes.

Formulas and variables

\[ V = B h \qquad L = P h \qquad T = 2B + P h \]
SymbolMeaning
\(B\)Area of one base.
\(P\)Perimeter of one base.
\(h\)Height of the prism: the distance between the bases (its "length" when lying down).
\(L, T, V\)Lateral area, total surface area and volume.

Base areas used: rectangle \(B = lw\); triangle by Heron's formula \(B = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}\) with \(s = \tfrac{a+b+c}{2}\); regular \(n\)-gon with side \(a\): \(B = \dfrac{n a^2}{4\tan(180^\circ/n)}\).

Worked example

A triangular prism with base sides 3, 4, 5 cm and length 10 cm

Step 1 — Base area (Heron's formula)

\[ s = \frac{3 + 4 + 5}{2} = 6, \qquad B = \sqrt{6(3)(2)(1)} = \sqrt{36} = 6 \text{ cm}^2 \]

Step 2 — Perimeter and lateral area

\[ P = 12 \text{ cm}, \qquad L = P h = 12 \times 10 = 120 \text{ cm}^2 \]

Step 3 — Total area and volume

\[ T = 2(6) + 120 = 132 \text{ cm}^2, \qquad V = 6 \times 10 = 60 \text{ cm}^3 \]

Assumptions, domain and rounding

  • Only right prisms are supported (side edges perpendicular to the bases).
  • Supported bases: rectangle, square (cube), any triangle, and regular polygons with 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10 or 12 sides.
  • Triangle sides must satisfy the triangle inequality; otherwise no triangle exists and no result is shown.
  • Regular-polygon and Heron results usually involve square roots or trigonometric values, so they are shown as decimals rounded to 6 places.
