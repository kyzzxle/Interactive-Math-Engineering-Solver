What the vector resultant calculator does

A vector has a size (magnitude) and a direction — forces, velocities and displacements are vectors. To add vectors, split each into horizontal and vertical components, add the components, then rebuild the resultant.

Drawn tip to tail, the resultant is the arrow from the start of the first vector to the end of the last.

Try it

Make the vectors perpendicular with sizes 3 and 4 in the explorer: the resultant has size 5.

Formulas and variables

\[ V_x = |V|\cos\theta \qquad V_y = |V|\sin\theta \]\[ R_x = \sum V_x,\quad R_y = \sum V_y \qquad |R| = \sqrt{R_x^2 + R_y^2},\quad \theta_R = \operatorname{atan2}(R_y, R_x) \]
SymbolMeaning
\(|V|,\ \theta\)Magnitude and direction (counter-clockwise from the positive x-axis)
\(R_x,\ R_y\)Components of the resultant
\(\operatorname{atan2}\)Inverse tangent that keeps track of the quadrant

Worked example

10 units at 30° plus 6 units at 120°

Step 1 — Components

\[ (10\cos 30^\circ,\ 10\sin 30^\circ) \approx (8.660,\ 5),\quad (6\cos 120^\circ,\ 6\sin 120^\circ) \approx (-3,\ 5.196) \]

Step 2 — Add

\[ R \approx (5.660,\ 10.196) \]

Step 3 — Magnitude and direction

\[ |R| \approx \sqrt{5.660^2 + 10.196^2} \approx 11.66,\quad \theta \approx 60.96^\circ \]

Domain, special cases and common mistakes

  • Up to 6 vectors. Magnitudes must be non-negative; directions in degrees.
  • If the components cancel, the resultant is the zero vector and has no direction.

Common mistakes

  • Using tan⁻¹(Ry/Rx) without checking the quadrant (it is wrong whenever Rx < 0).
  • Adding magnitudes directly instead of components.
