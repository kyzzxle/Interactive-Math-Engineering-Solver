What the composite solid calculator does

Many real objects are built from simple solids: a grain silo is a cylinder with a dome, a pill is a cylinder with two half-spheres, a pencil tip is a cone on a cylinder. Their volume is the sum of the parts.

Surface area needs more care: where two parts are joined, the joining circle is inside the object and is not part of the surface. This calculator counts only the faces you could paint.

Try it

Compare the silo with a plain cylinder of the same total height: the dome holds less than the cylinder slice it replaces.

Formulas and variables

\[ V_{\text{cyl}} = \pi r^2 h \qquad V_{\text{cone}} = \tfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2 H \qquad V_{\text{hemi}} = \tfrac{2}{3}\pi r^3 \]\[ A_{\text{side}} = 2\pi r h \qquad A_{\text{cone}} = \pi r s,\ s = \sqrt{r^2 + H^2} \qquad A_{\text{dome}} = 2\pi r^2 \]
SymbolMeaning
\(r\)Common radius of the parts
\(h\)Height (length) of the cylinder
\(H\)Height of the cone
\(r_i\)Inner radius of a tube

Worked example

A silo: cylinder 10 m tall with a hemispherical roof, r = 3 m

Step 1 — Volume

\[ V = \pi(3)^2(10) + \tfrac{2}{3}\pi(3)^3 = 90\pi + 18\pi = 108\pi \approx 339.29\ \text{m}^3 \]

Step 2 — Exposed surfaces

\[ A = 2\pi(3)(10) + 2\pi(3)^2 + \pi(3)^2 = 60\pi + 18\pi + 9\pi = 87\pi \approx 273.32\ \text{m}^2 \]

Note

The circle where the roof meets the cylinder is not counted, but the floor is.

Domain, special cases and common mistakes

  • All parts share the same radius and are joined along complete circles.
  • For a tube, the inner radius must be smaller than the outer radius.
  • Five shapes are supported: silo, capsule, rocket, ice-cream cone and tube.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the surface areas of the separate parts, which wrongly includes the hidden joining faces.
  • Using the cone’s height instead of its slant height for the curved area.
