What the shoelace formula calculator does

The shoelace formula (also called the surveyor’s or Gauss’s area formula) gives the area of any polygon directly from the coordinates of its vertices, listed in order around the boundary.

Writing the coordinates in two columns and cross-multiplying diagonally looks like lacing a shoe — hence the name. The sign of the sum tells you whether the vertices go counter-clockwise (positive) or clockwise (negative).

Try it

In the explorer, drag one corner across the opposite side. The polygon crosses itself and the formula no longer gives the enclosed area.

Formulas and variables

\[ A = \frac{1}{2}\left\lvert \sum_{i=1}^{n} \left(x_i y_{i+1} - x_{i+1} y_i\right) \right\rvert,\quad (x_{n+1}, y_{n+1}) = (x_1, y_1) \]
SymbolMeaning
\((x_i, y_i)\)Vertices in order around the polygon
\(n\)Number of vertices
\(A\)Area

The centroid is \(\left(\frac{1}{6A_s}\sum (x_i + x_{i+1})c_i,\ \frac{1}{6A_s}\sum (y_i + y_{i+1})c_i\right)\) with \(c_i = x_i y_{i+1} - x_{i+1} y_i\) and \(A_s\) the signed area.

Worked example

Quadrilateral (0, 0), (4, 0), (4, 3), (0, 5)

Step 1 — Cross products

\[ 0 \cdot 0 - 4 \cdot 0 = 0,\quad 4 \cdot 3 - 4 \cdot 0 = 12,\quad 4 \cdot 5 - 0 \cdot 3 = 20,\quad 0 \cdot 0 - 0 \cdot 5 = 0 \]

Step 2 — Add

\[ 0 + 12 + 20 + 0 = 32 \]

Step 3 — Halve

\[ A = \tfrac{1}{2}|32| = 16 \]

Domain, special cases and common mistakes

  • At least 3 and at most 30 vertices, listed in order around the boundary (either direction).
  • The polygon must not cross itself; crossing sides are detected and flagged.
  • Collinear points (zero area) are rejected.

Common mistakes

  • Listing the vertices out of order, which makes the sides cross.
  • Forgetting the final term that wraps from the last vertex back to the first.
