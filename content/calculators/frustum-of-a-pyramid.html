What the frustum of a pyramid calculator does

A frustum of a pyramid is what remains when a pyramid is cut by a plane parallel to its base and the top is removed. It has two similar, parallel polygon bases and trapezoidal side faces — the shape of many hoppers, planters, dams and pedestal footings. The calculator handles frustums with regular polygon bases (square, hexagon and others) and with rectangular bases.

Try changing the input

Set the top edge equal to the bottom edge and you get a prism; set the top edge to 0 and you get a complete pyramid. The volume formula covers both cases.

Formulas and variables

\[ V = \frac{h}{3}\left(A_1 + A_2 + \sqrt{A_1 A_2}\right) \qquad T = A_1 + A_2 + L \]
SymbolMeaning
\(A_1, A_2\)Areas of the bottom (larger) and top (smaller) bases.
\(h\)Perpendicular distance between the bases.
\(s\)Slant height of a trapezoidal face, \(s = \sqrt{h^2 + (m_1 - m_2)^2}\), where \(m_1, m_2\) are the apothems of the bases.
\(L\)Lateral area: for a regular \(n\)-gon, \(L = n\cdot\frac{a + b}{2}\cdot s\).

The volume formula comes from subtracting the small removed pyramid from the large original one; the \(\sqrt{A_1 A_2}\) term is the "average" that appears because the cross-sectional area changes quadratically with height.

Worked example

Square frustum: bottom edge 10 m, top edge 6 m, height 3 m

Step 1 — Base areas

\[ A_1 = 10^2 = 100, \qquad A_2 = 6^2 = 36, \qquad \sqrt{A_1 A_2} = 60 \]

Step 2 — Volume

\[ V = \frac{3}{3}(100 + 36 + 60) = 196 \text{ m}^3 \]

Step 3 — Slant height and lateral area

\[ s = \sqrt{3^2 + (5 - 3)^2} = \sqrt{13} \approx 3.6056, \qquad L = 4\cdot\frac{10 + 6}{2}\cdot\sqrt{13} = 32\sqrt{13} \approx 115.38 \text{ m}^2 \]

Step 4 — Total surface area

\[ T = 100 + 36 + 115.38 \approx 251.38 \text{ m}^2 \]

Assumptions, domain and rounding

  • The frustum comes from a right pyramid: the two bases are parallel, similar and centred on the same vertical axis.
  • Rectangular bases must have the same proportions (\(L_2/L_1 = W_2/W_1\)); otherwise the side faces would not meet at one apex and the solid is not a pyramid frustum. The calculator rejects such inputs and tells you the matching width.
  • The bottom base must be the larger one. A top edge of 0 gives a pyramid.
  • Results are rounded to 6 decimal places.
