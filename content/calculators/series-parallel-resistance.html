What the series and parallel resistance calculator does

Resistors can be joined in series (one after another, so the same current flows through each) or in parallel (side by side, so each has the same voltage across it). A whole group can be replaced by a single equivalent resistance that draws the same current from the supply.

This calculator handles series, parallel, and parallel branches where each branch contains resistors in series. Add a supply voltage to see the total current and the current, voltage and power for every resistor.

Quick check

A series total is always larger than the largest resistor. A parallel total is always smaller than the smallest resistor — adding another path makes it easier for current to flow.

Formulas and variables

\[ R_{\text{series}} = R_1 + R_2 + \cdots + R_n \qquad \frac{1}{R_{\text{parallel}}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + \cdots + \frac{1}{R_n} \]

For exactly two resistors in parallel, the reciprocal formula simplifies to "product over sum":

\[ R_{\text{parallel}} = \frac{R_1 R_2}{R_1 + R_2} \]

To enter parallel branches, separate the branches with semicolons. For example, 10, 20; 30 means a 10 Ω and a 20 Ω resistor in series, connected in parallel with a 30 Ω resistor.

Worked example

6 Ω and 3 Ω in parallel across 12 V

Step 1 — Equivalent resistance

\[ R_{\text{eq}} = \frac{6 \times 3}{6 + 3} = \frac{18}{9} = 2\ \Omega \]

Step 2 — Total current

\[ I = \frac{V}{R_{\text{eq}}} = \frac{12}{2} = 6\ \text{A} \]

Step 3 — Current in each branch

\[ I_1 = \frac{12}{6} = 2\ \text{A}, \qquad I_2 = \frac{12}{3} = 4\ \text{A} \]

The branch currents add up to the total: 2 A + 4 A = 6 A.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Wires have no resistance and the supply is ideal. All resistors are ohmic and every resistance must be greater than 0.
  • Supported layouts: all in series, all in parallel, or parallel branches that each contain resistors in series. Bridge circuits and networks that need Kirchhoff's laws are not supported.
  • Up to 20 resistors. Values are in ohms; enter 4.7k as 4700.
