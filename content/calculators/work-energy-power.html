What the work, energy and power calculator does

Work is energy transferred by a force acting through a distance. Kinetic energy is the energy of motion, gravitational potential energy is energy stored by height, and power is how quickly energy is transferred. Choose one of the five calculations and the page shows the formula, the substitution and what the answer means.

The fifth option uses conservation of energy: without friction or air resistance, the potential energy lost in a fall becomes kinetic energy, which gives the speed at the bottom without needing the time.

Formulas and variables

\[ W = F\,d\cos\theta \qquad KE = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2 \qquad PE = mgh \qquad P = \frac{W}{t} \]
\[ \tfrac{1}{2}mu^2 + mgh = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2 \;\Longrightarrow\; v = \sqrt{u^2 + 2gh} \]
SymbolMeaningSI unit
\(W\)Work or energy transferredjoules, J
\(F,\ d,\ \theta\)Force, displacement, angle between themN, m, degrees
\(m,\ v,\ h\)Mass, speed, heightkg, m/s, m
\(P,\ t\)Power, timewatts (W), s

Only the part of the force along the motion, \(F\cos\theta\), does work. A force at 90° to the motion — like gravity on a car driving on a flat road — does no work.

Worked example

How fast is a stone moving after falling 20 m from rest?

Step 1 — Known values

\(h = 20\) m, \(u = 0\), \(g = 9.81\) m/s².

Step 2 — Energy is conserved

\[ mgh = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2 \;\Longrightarrow\; v = \sqrt{2gh} \]

Step 3 — Substitute and simplify

\[ v = \sqrt{2(9.81)(20)} = \sqrt{392.4} \approx 19.81\ \text{m/s} \]

The mass cancels, so a heavy and a light stone reach the same speed in this model.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Forces are constant and act along a straight displacement. For varying forces, work is the area under a force–distance graph.
  • The energy-conservation option ignores air resistance and friction; real objects arrive slower because some energy becomes heat and sound.
  • \(PE = mgh\) assumes constant \(g\), accurate for heights much smaller than Earth's radius. Height is measured from a reference level you choose.
  • Power is the average over the time given. Kinetic energy uses the non-relativistic formula, valid far below the speed of light.
