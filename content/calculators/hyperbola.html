What the hyperbola calculator does

A hyperbola is the set of points whose distances to two foci differ by a constant, 2a. It has two separate branches, each curving around one focus, and far from the centre each branch approaches a straight line, an asymptote.

Here \(a\) is the semi-axis in the direction the hyperbola opens (centre to vertex) and \(b\) the conjugate semi-axis, which sets the slopes of the asymptotes.

Try it

Make a = b in the explorer: the asymptotes are perpendicular (a rectangular hyperbola, like y = 1/x turned 45°).

Formulas and variables

\[ \frac{(x - h)^2}{a^2} - \frac{(y - k)^2}{b^2} = 1 \quad \text{or} \quad \frac{(y - k)^2}{a^2} - \frac{(x - h)^2}{b^2} = 1 \]\[ c^2 = a^2 + b^2 \qquad e = \frac{c}{a} > 1 \qquad y - k = \pm\frac{b}{a}(x - h) \]
SymbolMeaning
\((h, k)\)Centre

For a hyperbola that opens up and down, the asymptotes have slopes ±a/b instead.

Worked example

Opens left–right, centre (1, −2), a = 3, b = 4

Step 1 — Equation

\[ \frac{(x - 1)^2}{9} - \frac{(y + 2)^2}{16} = 1 \]

Step 2 — Foci

\[ c^2 = 9 + 16 = 25,\ c = 5:\quad (1 \pm 5,\ -2) \]

Step 3 — Asymptotes and eccentricity

\[ y + 2 = \pm \tfrac{4}{3}(x - 1),\qquad e = \tfrac{5}{3} \]

Domain, special cases and common mistakes

  • a and b must be positive; b may be larger than a.
  • Only axis-aligned hyperbolas (no rotation) are supported.

Common mistakes

  • Using c² = a² − b² as for an ellipse.
  • Swapping a and b when the hyperbola opens vertically.
