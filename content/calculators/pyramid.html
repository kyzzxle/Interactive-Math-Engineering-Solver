What is a pyramid?

A pyramid has a polygon base and triangular side faces that meet at a single point, the apex. In a right pyramid the apex is directly above the centre of the base. The Egyptian pyramids are square pyramids; a hip roof and a church spire are pyramids too.

Like a prism, a pyramid is named by its base: triangular, square, rectangular, pentagonal, hexagonal and so on. A triangular pyramid whose faces are all equilateral is a regular tetrahedron.

What this calculator does

Choose a regular base (3 to 12 equal sides) or a rectangular base, and enter its edges and the height. The calculator finds:

  • the base area, using the apothem for regular bases,
  • the slant height of each face (two different ones for a rectangle),
  • the lateral area, the total surface area and the volume,
  • the lateral edge from the apex to a corner.

The geometry behind it

A pyramid problem involves three different right triangles inside the solid:

  • Height, apothem, slant height: from the centre of the base to the middle of an edge (apothem \(m\)) and up to the apex, \(s = \sqrt{h^2 + m^2}\). This gives the face heights.
  • Height, circumradius, lateral edge: from the centre to a corner and up, \(e = \sqrt{h^2 + R^2}\). This gives the edge lengths.
  • Half an edge, slant height, lateral edge on each face: \(e^2 = s^2 + (a/2)^2\).

One third of a prism

Three pyramids of equal volume can be assembled into the prism with the same base and height. So a pyramid holds exactly one third as much: \(V = \tfrac{1}{3}Bh\).

Deriving the formulas

Volume by slicing

At distance \(y\) below the apex, a horizontal slice is the base shrunk by the factor \(y/h\), so its area is \(B\,(y/h)^2\). Adding the slices:

\[ V = \int_0^h B\frac{y^2}{h^2}\,dy = \frac{B}{h^2}\cdot\frac{h^3}{3} = \frac{1}{3}Bh. \]

Lateral area of a regular pyramid

All \(n\) faces are congruent isosceles triangles with base \(a\) and height \(s\):

\[ L = n\cdot\tfrac{1}{2}a s = \tfrac{1}{2}(na)s = \tfrac{1}{2}Ps. \]

Complete worked example

Problem

A square pyramid has base edges of 6 m and a height of 4 m. Find the slant height, the surface area and the volume.

Step 1: Base area and apothem

\[ B = 6^2 = 36\ \text{m}^2, \qquad m = \tfrac{6}{2} = 3\ \text{m} \]

Step 2: Slant height

\[ s = \sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{25} = 5\ \text{m} \]

Step 3: Lateral and total area

\[ L = \tfrac{1}{2}(4 \times 6)(5) = 60\ \text{m}^2, \qquad T = 36 + 60 = 96\ \text{m}^2 \]

Step 4: Volume

\[ V = \tfrac{1}{3}(36)(4) = 48\ \text{m}^3 \]

Step 5: Lateral edge

\[ R = 3\sqrt{2} \approx 4.2426, \qquad e = \sqrt{4^2 + 18} = \sqrt{34} \approx 5.831\ \text{m} \]

Second worked example: a rectangular base

Problem

A hip roof is a pyramid on a 10 m × 6 m rectangle, rising 4 m. Find the roof area.

Step 1: Slant heights

\[ s_l = \sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = 5\ \text{m}\ \ (\text{faces on the 10 m edges}), \qquad s_w = \sqrt{4^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{41} \approx 6.403\ \text{m} \]

Step 2: Roof area

\[ L = 10(5) + 6(6.403) \approx 50 + 38.42 = 88.42\ \text{m}^2 \]

Answer

The roof covers about 88.4 m². The two triangles on the long edges are 25 m² each and the two on the short edges are about 19.2 m² each.

Practical applications

  • Roofs. Hip roofs and spires: roofing area is the lateral area.
  • Monuments and architecture. Volumes of stone; glass for pyramidal skylights.
  • Packaging and tents. Pyramidal boxes and tents.
  • Stockpiles on a rectangular footprint.

Common mistakes

  1. Using the slant height in the volume. Volume needs the perpendicular height.
  2. Confusing slant height and lateral edge. The slant height meets the middle of a base edge; the lateral edge meets a corner, and is longer.
  3. Forgetting the 1/3.
  4. One slant height for a rectangle. A rectangular pyramid has two different face heights.
  5. Adding two bases. A pyramid has only one.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The pyramid is right. For a leaning pyramid, the volume formula still holds with the perpendicular height, but the face areas differ.
  • For a pyramid with its top cut off, use the frustum of a pyramid calculator.
  • Regular bases have 3 to 12 equal sides.

Frequently asked questions

What is the volume of a pyramid?

One third of the base area times the height: V = (1/3)Bh.

What is the slant height of a pyramid?

The height of a triangular face, from the middle of a base edge to the apex: √(h² + m²), where m is the apothem.

How do I find the height from the slant height?

h = √(s² − m²). For a square pyramid, m is half the base edge.

Is a tetrahedron a pyramid?

Yes, a triangular pyramid. If all four faces are equilateral it is a regular tetrahedron; see the regular polyhedron calculator.

Why is the volume one third of a prism?

A cube can be cut into three identical pyramids that meet at one corner, and slicing shows the same one-third factor for any base.
