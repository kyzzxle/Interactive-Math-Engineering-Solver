What the linear regression calculator does

A least-squares regression line is the straight line that best fits paired data: it makes the sum of squared vertical distances (residuals) from the points to the line as small as possible.

Pearson’s correlation \(r\) measures how close the points are to a straight line, from −1 (perfect negative) through 0 (no linear pattern) to +1 (perfect positive). \(r^2\) is the share of the variation in y explained by the line.

Try it

Drag one point far away in the explorer: a single outlier can tilt the line and slash r.

Formulas and variables

\[ b = \frac{S_{xy}}{S_{xx}},\quad a = \bar{y} - b\bar{x},\quad \hat{y} = a + bx \]\[ r = \frac{S_{xy}}{\sqrt{S_{xx}S_{yy}}} \]
SymbolMeaning
\(S_{xx},\ S_{yy},\ S_{xy}\)Sums of squared deviations and of products of deviations from the means
\(a,\ b\)Intercept and slope
\(r,\ r^2\)Correlation and coefficient of determination

Worked example

Points (1, 2), (2, 3), (3, 5)

Step 1 — Means

\[ \bar{x} = 2,\quad \bar{y} = \tfrac{10}{3} \]

Step 2 — Sums

\[ S_{xx} = 2,\quad S_{xy} = (-1)(-\tfrac{4}{3}) + 0 + (1)(\tfrac{5}{3}) = 3 \]

Step 3 — Line

\[ b = \tfrac{3}{2},\quad a = \tfrac{10}{3} - \tfrac{3}{2}(2) = \tfrac{1}{3},\quad \hat{y} = \tfrac{1}{3} + 1.5x \]

Domain, special cases and common mistakes

  • At least 3 pairs (up to 1,000). The x-values cannot all be equal.
  • Predictions outside the range of the data (extrapolation) are flagged.

Common mistakes

  • Reading correlation as causation.
  • Trusting r without looking at the scatter plot (curves and outliers can mislead).
