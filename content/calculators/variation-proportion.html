What is variation?

Variation describes how one quantity changes with others. If \(y\) varies directly as \(x\), then \(y = kx\): doubling \(x\) doubles \(y\). If \(y\) varies inversely as \(x\), then \(y = \tfrac{k}{x}\): doubling \(x\) halves \(y\). The number \(k\) is the constant of variation, and one known pair of values is enough to find it.

Try changing the input

Choose "direct" with power 2. Then tripling \(x\) multiplies \(y\) by \(3^2 = 9\), whatever the value of \(k\).

What this calculator does

  • Variation: direct \(y = kx^n\), inverse \(y = \tfrac{k}{x^n}\), joint \(y = kx^nw^m\) and combined \(y = \tfrac{kx^n}{w^m}\), with powers 1, 2, 3 or ½. From one known set of values it finds \(k\), then the new \(y\), and checks it with ratios.
  • Proportion: the fourth, mean and third proportional.
  • Divide in a ratio: shares a total in the ratio \(r_1 : r_2\) or \(r_1 : r_2 : r_3\).

The idea behind it

Every variation statement translates into an equation with one unknown constant:

  • "\(y\) varies directly as the square of \(x\)" → \(y = kx^2\).
  • "\(y\) varies inversely as \(x\)" → \(y = \tfrac{k}{x}\), so \(xy = k\) stays constant.
  • "\(z\) varies jointly as \(x\) and \(y\)" → \(z = kxy\).
  • "\(z\) varies directly as \(x\) and inversely as \(y^2\)" → \(z = \tfrac{kx}{y^2}\).

The ratio shortcut

Dividing two instances of the same law cancels \(k\): for \(y = kx^2\), \(\tfrac{y_2}{y_1} = \left(\tfrac{x_2}{x_1}\right)^2\). This is often the quickest route.

Variables explained

SymbolMeaning
\(y\)The quantity that varies.
\(x, w\)The quantities it depends on.
\(n, m\)Powers: 1 for "as x", 2 for "as the square of x", ½ for "as the square root of x".
\(k\)The constant of variation, the same for every set of values.
\(x_1, y_1\)The known set; \(x_2, y_2\) the new set.

The method

  1. Translate the statement into an equation with \(k\).
  2. Substitute the known set of values and solve for \(k\).
  3. Write the equation with that value of \(k\).
  4. Substitute the new values to find the unknown.

For a proportion \(a : b = c : x\), write it as \(\tfrac{a}{b} = \tfrac{c}{x}\) and cross-multiply: \(ax = bc\). For the mean proportional, \(a : x = x : b\) gives \(x^2 = ab\).

Complete worked example

Problem

\(y\) varies directly as the square of \(x\), and \(y = 12\) when \(x = 2\). Find \(y\) when \(x = 5\).

Step 1: Equation

\[ y = kx^2 \]

Step 2: Find k

\[ 12 = k(2)^2 \;\Rightarrow\; k = 3 \]

Step 3: New value

\[ y = 3(5)^2 = 75 \]

Check with the ratio

\(\tfrac{y_2}{12} = \left(\tfrac{5}{2}\right)^2 = 6.25\), so \(y_2 = 75\). ✓

Second worked example: combined variation

Problem

The safe load \(L\) of a beam varies directly as its width \(x\) and inversely as the square of its length \(w\). \(L = 9\) kN when \(x = 6\) cm and \(w = 2\) m. Find \(L\) when \(x = 10\) cm and \(w = 5\) m.

Step 1: Equation and k

\[ L = \frac{kx}{w^2} \;\Rightarrow\; 9 = \frac{k(6)}{2^2} \;\Rightarrow\; k = 6 \]

Step 2: New load

\[ L = \frac{6(10)}{5^2} = \frac{60}{25} = 2.4 \text{ kN} \]

Check

Width × \(\tfrac{10}{6}\) and length × \(\tfrac{5}{2}\): \(9 \times \tfrac{10}{6} \div \left(\tfrac{5}{2}\right)^2 = 2.4\). ✓

Practical applications

  • Physics: gravity and light intensity vary inversely as the square of the distance; kinetic energy jointly as mass and the square of speed.
  • Structures: beam deflection and load capacity depend on powers of the span and depth.
  • Electricity: wire resistance varies directly as length and inversely as cross-sectional area.
  • Gas laws: Boyle's law \(PV = k\) is inverse variation.
  • Sharing costs and profits in a ratio.

Common mistakes

  1. Forgetting the power. "As the square of x" means \(x^2\), not \(x\).
  2. Mixing up direct and inverse. Inverse means \(k\) divided by the variable.
  3. Using a different k for the new values. k is found once and stays fixed.
  4. Mixing units. Keep each quantity in the same unit in both sets.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Powers are 1, 2, 3 or ½. Answers are exact fractions for whole-number powers.
  • A quantity in a denominator cannot be 0, and the known value of y cannot be 0.
  • Ratio parts must be positive.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the constant of variation?

Write the variation as an equation, substitute one known set of values, and solve for k.

What is the difference between direct and inverse variation?

In direct variation y and x increase together (y = kx). In inverse variation one increases as the other decreases, and their product stays constant (xy = k).

What is joint variation?

One quantity varies as the product of two or more others, z = kxy.

What is the mean proportional of two numbers?

The number x with a : x = x : b, which is √(ab). For 4 and 9 it is 6.
