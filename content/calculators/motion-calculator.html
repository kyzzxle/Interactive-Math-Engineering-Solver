What the speed, distance and time calculator does

When something moves at a constant speed, the distance it covers grows by the same amount every second. Speed, distance and time are then linked by one simple relationship, and knowing any two of them gives the third. This calculator rearranges the formula for whichever quantity you choose and shows every substitution.

It also distinguishes speed from velocity. Speed is how fast something moves and is never negative. Velocity also has a direction: along a straight line we pick one direction as positive, so a velocity of −3 m/s means 3 m/s in the opposite direction. The matching quantity is displacement (change in position) rather than distance (length of the path).

Formulas and variables

\[ v = \frac{d}{t} \qquad d = v\,t \qquad t = \frac{d}{v} \]
SymbolMeaningSI unit
\(v\)Speed (or velocity, with a sign for direction)metres per second, m/s
\(d\)Distance travelled (or displacement)metres, m
\(t\)Time takenseconds, s

To convert a speed from m/s to km/h, multiply by 3.6, because in one hour (3600 s) an object at 1 m/s covers 3600 m, which is 3.6 km: \(1\ \text{m/s} = 3.6\ \text{km/h}\). To go from km/h to m/s, divide by 3.6.

On a position–time graph, constant velocity is a straight line and the slope of the line equals the velocity.

Worked example

A runner completes 400 m in 50 s

Step 1 — Known values

\(d = 400\) m and \(t = 50\) s. We want the average speed \(v\).

Step 2 — Formula

\[ v = \frac{d}{t} \]

Step 3 — Substitute and simplify

\[ v = \frac{400\ \text{m}}{50\ \text{s}} = 8\ \text{m/s} \]

Step 4 — Convert

\[ 8\ \text{m/s} \times 3.6 = 28.8\ \text{km/h} \]

Assumptions, domain and limitations

  • The speed is constant over the whole time interval. If it changes, the result is the average speed, and the kinematics calculator handles constant acceleration.
  • Velocity mode assumes motion along one straight line. Displacement and velocity must have the same sign when solving for time; otherwise the object moves away and never arrives.
  • Time must be greater than 0. Speed mode needs non-negative distances and speeds.
  • Enter values in metres and seconds. Decimals are rounded to 6 places and marked "approx." when not exact.
