What the limit calculator does

The limit of \(f(x)\) as \(x\) approaches \(a\) is the value \(f(x)\) gets closer and closer to — whatever happens exactly at \(a\). Limits describe holes, jumps, asymptotes and long-run behaviour, and they underpin derivatives and integrals.

This calculator first tries direct substitution, then cancels a 0/0 form exactly for ratios of polynomials, and otherwise estimates the limit from a table of values on each side. Numerical estimates are always labelled as such.

Formulas and variables

\[ \lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L \iff \lim_{x \to a^-} f(x) = \lim_{x \to a^+} f(x) = L \]\[ \lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{a_n x^n + \cdots}{b_m x^m + \cdots} = \begin{cases} 0, & n < m \\ a_n / b_m, & n = m \\ \pm\infty, & n > m \end{cases} \]
SymbolMeaning
\(a\)The point x approaches (a number, or ±∞)
\(a^-,\ a^+\)Approaching from the left or from the right
\(L\)The limit

Worked example

Find the limit of (x² − 9)/(x − 3) as x → 3

Step 1 — Substitute

\[ \frac{9 - 9}{3 - 3} = \frac{0}{0}\ \text{(indeterminate)} \]

Step 2 — Factor and cancel

\[ \frac{(x - 3)(x + 3)}{x - 3} = x + 3 \quad (x \ne 3) \]

Step 3 — Substitute again

\[ \lim_{x \to 3} (x + 3) = 6 \]

Domain, special cases and common mistakes

  • Functions of x using +, −, ×, ÷, powers, roots, exponentials, logarithms and trig functions.
  • Numerical estimates sample down to 10⁻⁶ from the point; functions that change faster than that (or oscillate) are reported as having no limit.

Common mistakes

  • Concluding a limit does not exist just because f(a) is undefined.
  • Ignoring that the left and right limits must agree for a two-sided limit.
