Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information is involved when you use Interactive Math & Engineering Solver Hub ("Solver Hub", "the site", "we", "us"), why, and what choices you have. The short version: the calculators run entirely in your browser and we do not ask you to create an account. Third-party services that deliver the site and, where enabled, its advertising may process limited technical data as described below.

Summary

  • Numbers you type into a calculator are processed on your own device and are not sent to us.
  • We store one small preference in your browser: whether you chose light or dark mode.
  • If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads, including personalised ads where permitted. You can control this — see Advertising.
  • If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send.

Information we handle

Calculator inputs

All calculations are performed by JavaScript in your browser. The values you enter are not transmitted to a server operated by us. After you press Calculate, the inputs are added to the page address (for example ?r=6&R=9) so that you can bookmark or share a solution. That address can be stored in your browser history and is sent to the hosting provider if you reload or share the link, in the same way as any web address.

Preferences stored on your device

When you switch between light and dark mode, the choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the key theme. It never leaves your device and you can remove it by clearing your browser's site data.

Information you send us

If you use the contact form or email us, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, email address and message. We use them only to respond to you and to improve the site, and we do not sell them. The contact form may be delivered through a third-party form-processing service, which handles the message under its own privacy policy.

Technical data collected by service providers

Like every website, Solver Hub cannot be delivered without your device connecting to servers. These providers may receive and log technical information such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request:

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • No third-party content networks: page styling, scripts and mathematical typesetting are all served from this website itself, so loading a page does not contact any other company's servers (unless advertising is enabled, as described above).
  • Advertising: Google, when advertising is enabled (see below).

We do not use separate analytics or tracking tools beyond those listed here. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

Advertising and cookies

Solver Hub may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense to help cover its costs. When advertising is active:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Other third-party vendors or ad networks may also use cookies to serve ads on this site.
  • Google and its partners may also use cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies to limit how often you see an ad, measure ad performance and protect against fraud.

You can learn more and control how these technologies are used:

  • Opt out of personalised advertising from Google in Google Ads Settings (My Ad Center).
  • Read how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and how Google uses cookies in advertising.
  • Opt out of personalised advertising from many participating third-party vendors at www.aboutads.info/choices (United States) or www.youronlinechoices.eu (Europe).
  • Block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies does not stop ads from appearing, but they may be less relevant.

Consent and your choices

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent message from a Google-certified consent management platform is shown before personalised advertising cookies are used, where advertising is enabled. You can accept, reject or customise your choices, and you can change them later using the Privacy choices link in the footer (shown when advertising is active).

Residents of some U.S. states have rights under state privacy laws, including the right to opt out of the use of personal information for targeted advertising. You can exercise that choice with the controls listed above or by contacting us. We do not sell personal information in exchange for money.

Legal bases (EEA and UK visitors)

Where data protection law such as the GDPR applies, we rely on: your consent for advertising cookies; our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the site for technical data processed by our hosting and content providers; and your request when we reply to messages you send.

Children's privacy

Solver Hub is an educational resource intended for high-school students, college students and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Retention and security

We keep messages only as long as needed to respond and to maintain a record of corrections made to the site, then delete them. The site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit the information we handle to what is described in this policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we hold very little personal information — usually only messages you have sent — the simplest way to exercise these rights is to contact us.

Links to other websites

The site links to external resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites, and we encourage you to read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when a new service is added. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the contact page and choose "Privacy request" as the topic.
